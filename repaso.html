Presente · Pretérito · Imperfecto — repaso para Brinkley

The Big Rule: Photo vs. Video

📸

Pretérito = a photo

Click. It happened once. It's done.

Ayer fui al cine.
Yesterday I went to the movies.

🎬

Imperfecto = a video

Playing in the background. What things were like, what used to happen.

Cuando era niña, iba al cine todos los sábados.

Signal words — they tell you which tense before you conjugate

📸 Photo → Pretérito
  • ayer yesterday
  • anoche last night
  • una vez one time
  • de repente suddenly
  • el año pasado last year
  • primero... luego... first... then...
🎬 Video → Imperfecto
  • siempre always
  • todos los días every day
  • cuando era niña when I was little
  • mientras while
  • generalmente usually
  • de niña as a kid

🎬 Imperfecto has only 3 irregulars in the whole language: ser → era · ir → iba · ver → veía. Everything else follows the song. That's it. Three verbs.
