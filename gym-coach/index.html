    Strength — built for osteoporosis, not around it

    Bones get stronger from load and impact done with a neutral spine. The rule: hinge at the hips, never round the back under weight, no loaded crunches or toe-touches, no heavy twisting.

    DO — LOADS BONE

    • A: Goblet squats, dumbbell Romanian deadlifts (hips back, flat back), farmer carries down the hallway, step-ups
    • B: Overhead press, bent-over rows (chest supported on bench), suitcase carries, heel drops / small hops for impact

    SKIP OR SWAP

    • Sit-ups, crunches, rounded-back deadlifts → swap for planks, dead bugs, bird dogs
    • Loaded Russian twists, aggressive yoga forward folds → swap for pallof presses (band anti-rotation)

    Yoga & stretching — with an osteoporosis filter

    Yoga is great for you — but the classic flow includes moves that load a rounded spine, which is the one thing your bones shouldn't do. Same rule as strength: extension yes, loaded flexion and deep twists no.

    GOOD FOR YOU

    • Warrior I & II, chair pose — leg strength, upright spine
    • Bridge pose — back extension, glutes, posture
    • Cobra / sphinx (gentle) — directly counters the hunch
    • Tree, balance poses — fall prevention, which is the real osteoporosis game
    • Hip flexor lunge stretch, figure-4, lying hamstring stretch with a strap — runner maintenance with a flat back
    • Cat-cow moved gently, doorway chest stretch, child's pose kept easy

    SKIP OR SOFTEN

    • Deep seated or standing forward folds (rounding under gravity) → hinge at hips with a long spine instead
    • Plow, shoulder stand, deep spinal twists → skip
    • Anything where you round hard and pull → soften it

    A 20-minute gentle or Iyengar-style class works; just substitute when the instructor calls a deep fold. Peloton has yoga and stretch classes too — those count as this.

    Posture — the un-hunch kit

    A hunched upper back responds to daily extension work, not willpower. 5–10 minutes, most days:

    • Wall angels — 2×10, slow, back flat to the wall
    • Band pull-aparts — 2×15 with a light band
    • Doorway chest stretch — 30s each side
    • Chin tucks — 10, at red lights or on Zoom
    • Prone Y-T-W raises on the floor or bench — 8 each

    Bonus: every strong row and carry above is also posture work. Pushing Diego's chair with tall shoulders is posture work too.
